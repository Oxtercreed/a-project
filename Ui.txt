<div class="legend">
<span class="lt">Design notes</span>
Palette is deep teal + marigold — teal reads as trust and finance, marigold nods to East African market energy and cash. <b>Every monetary figure across the app uses a monospace face</b> (feed captions excluded), so money always reads like a receipt line, whether it's in a chat bubble, an invoice, or the dashboard — a visible thread tying the social layer back to the POS core. The invoice card keeps its dashed "tear-off" edge on purpose. Cards use soft layered shadows instead of hard borders for a calmer, more premium feel, and the bottom navigation stays fixed at four tabs — <b>Feed, Chats, Dashboard, Tools</b> — matching the product spec exactly.
